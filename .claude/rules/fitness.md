# Fitness Domain Rules

- Treat training logic as real fitness programming, not generic content generation.
- Consider training goal, experience level, frequency, volume, intensity, recovery and exercise selection.
- Distinguish strength, hypertrophy, endurance and conditioning appropriately.
- Avoid recommending unnecessary or unsafe training practices.
- Do not assume that more volume or intensity is always better.
- Consider recovery and fatigue when designing repeated training sessions.
- Beginners and advanced users should not automatically receive identical programming.
- When load, RPE or progression data is unavailable, do not invent it.
- Clearly distinguish existing implemented fitness logic from proposed improvements.
- When modifying training algorithms, explain the training rationale before implementation.
