# Fitness Review

Evaluate fitness-related functionality using practical evidence-based training principles.

## Evaluate

Consider:

- Training goal: strength, hypertrophy, endurance, conditioning or general fitness.
- User experience level.
- Training frequency.
- Weekly and session volume.
- Intensity and effort.
- Sets, repetitions and rest periods.
- Exercise selection and movement patterns.
- Recovery and accumulated fatigue.
- Progressive overload.
- Load, reps and RPE when those data exist.
- Session structure and exercise order.
- Whether the programming is realistic for the target user.

## Rules

- Do not invent user data, training history or performance metrics.
- Do not assume more volume or intensity is always better.
- Do not treat generic exercise lists as complete training programs.
- Distinguish current implementation from recommended improvements.
- Flag potentially unsafe, unrealistic or poorly programmed training.
- Consider beginner, intermediate and advanced users when relevant.
- Prefer simple, explainable training logic over arbitrary formulas.
- Do not introduce medical claims or diagnose users.
- If required training data does not exist, explicitly identify the missing data.

## Output

When reviewing a fitness feature, report:

1. Current implementation.
2. What is correct.
3. Problems or limitations.
4. Why they matter.
5. Recommended improvement.
6. Data or architecture implications.

Do not modify files unless explicitly requested.
